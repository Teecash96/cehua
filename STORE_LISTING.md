## Full description

Cehua transforms raw product ideas into structured PRDs and visual user-flow diagrams — instantly.

Stop staring at blank documents. Describe your idea in plain language and Cehua generates a concise, editable PRD with problem statements, target users, features, user stories, and success criteria. You also get a visual user-flow diagram showing the complete journey from entry point to outcome.

Every section is fully editable. Assign team members to features and flow steps. Export as Markdown or copy as formatted text. All plans are saved locally on your Mac.

Whether you're a founder scoping your next product, a PM drafting a first pass, or a developer planning a side project — Cehua is where every idea starts.

## Feature bullets

- **AI-powered PRD generation** — structured, honest output with no made-up statistics
- **Visual user-flow diagrams** — connected cards showing the complete user journey
- **Team assignment** — add members and assign ownership to features and flow steps
- **Plan templates** — start from SaaS, Mobile App, Internal Tool, or API templates
- **Regenerate individual sections** — refine one part without regenerating the whole PRD
- **Export anywhere** — copy as text or export as Markdown
- **Local-first** — works offline, all data stays on your Mac
- **Light & dark mode** — adaptive design that feels native to macOS
- **Keyboard shortcuts** — built for speed
